# ADR-0013: URL Structure, Slug Immutability, and Sitemap Strategy

## Status
Accepted

## Context
In V8 (SEO & Discoverability), the Ethiopia News Aggregation Platform requires robust search engine optimization, machine-readable structured metadata, and stable URL hierarchies for all aggregated news events.

The system architecture specification (`docs/architecture/platform-architecture.md`, Section 6) outlined the canonical URL pattern `/news/{category-slug}/{event-slug}-{event-id}`. Previously in V6/V7, event detail pages were accessible via `/events/{id}` without category or slug semantics.

Furthermore, event headlines generated by the AI enrichment pipeline (`ai_headline`) may be updated during re-enrichment runs triggered by subsequent source arrivals (per V4/V7). If canonical URLs were derived live from headlines on every request, URLs would constantly mutate, invalidating indexed search engine records, external backlinks, and social shares.

## Decision

1. **Canonical URL Pattern**:
   We adopt `/news/{category-slug}/{event-slug}-{event-id}` as the authoritative URL structure for news events.

2. **Slug Generation at Initial Enrichment and Permanent Immutability**:
   - Event slugs are generated once upon initial event enrichment using a normalized ASCII/Ethiopic alphanumeric slugifier and stored in `news_events.slug`.
   - Slugs are protected via `COALESCE(slug, $new_slug)` and pipeline invariants: once a slug is written to the database, it is **immutable** and will never be overwritten or recalculated, even if subsequent re-enrichments produce an updated `ai_headline`.
   - The trailing `{event-id}` ensures deterministic resolution and collision immunity while the slug provides human readability and keyword relevance.

3. **Indefinite 301 Permanent Redirects for Legacy URLs**:
   - The legacy `/events/{id}` route is retained as an HTTP 301 permanent redirect handler that dynamically resolves the event and issues a `301 Moved Permanently` to its canonical `/news/{category}/{slug}-{id}` URL.
   - Any URL with an outdated or mismatched category/slug component automatically redirects (301) to the canonical path.

4. **Schema.org NewsArticle JSON-LD Structured Data**:
   - Every canonical event page renders server-side `<script type="application/ld+json">` containing schema.org `NewsArticle` metadata.
   - In accordance with our attribution and AI transparency principles, the structured data strictly reflects real pipeline data: verified headline, summary, timestamps, category, and source channel citations (`isBasedOn`), without fabricating missing author/image data.

5. **Single Dynamic Sitemap & Robots.txt**:
   - We implement dynamic `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` endpoints in Next.js App Router.
   - Active events (`status = 'active'`) and category routes are included. Unverified posts in `needs_review` are strictly excluded from sitemaps to prevent indexing of unverified data.
   - Dedicated Google News sitemaps (`news-sitemap.xml`) and Google News Publisher Center submissions are noted for future expansion but explicitly deferred until higher publication volumes and editorial review processes are established.

## Consequences

### Positive
- **Search Engine Discoverability**: Deep indexing of news events with descriptive, keyword-rich URLs and schema.org `NewsArticle` semantic metadata.
- **Link Stability**: Slugs never mutate, ensuring permanent longevity for backlinks, bookmarks, and search index entries.
- **Zero Downtime for Existing Links**: All legacy `/events/{id}` links seamlessly transition to canonical URLs without breakage.
- **Social Sharing**: Full Open Graph and Twitter Card tags provide rich previews across social platforms and messaging clients.

### Trade-offs & Limitations
- **Slug Drift vs Headline Updates**: If an event's headline changes significantly upon re-enrichment, the URL slug will reflect the initial headline. This is an intentional trade-off to prioritize link immutability over transient title synchronization.
- **Dynamic Sitemap Generation**: Sitemap is generated dynamically on demand. As event volume scales past tens of thousands of records, sitemap caching and segmented index files (`sitemap-index.xml`) will be introduced.
